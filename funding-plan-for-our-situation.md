# Funding and credits: what fits OUR situation

Prepared 2026-10-15. Figures come mostly from third-party guides, so confirm on each official page before applying.

## Our situation (what I assumed from this conversation)
- Job-swipe web app, demo stage, static site (GitHub Pages), no real back-end yet.
- No company yet (no LLC, no EIN), no business email, name not final.
- Self-funded, budget under $1,000.
- Recruiting sign-ups at college campuses and job fairs.
- No investor, accelerator or incubator behind us yet.

## Step 0: this week, costs almost nothing
| Action | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Keep hosting on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages (free tiers) | $0 | Hosting costs are about zero right now, so credits save little until you have a back-end or traffic |
| Ask your campus entrepreneurship center, career center or incubator what they offer | $0 | A referral from a school or incubator is what unlocks the big $100,000+ credit tiers later |
| If anyone on the team is a verified student: GitHub Student Developer Pack | $0 | From my own knowledge (not researched here): free tools and credits for students. Personal, not company |

## Step 1: form the company (unlocks nearly everything below)
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| LLC in your state, EIN (free from the IRS) | about $50-$300 |
| Domain with business email | about $20-$35 / year |
| Live website with the real name and a privacy notice | $0 |

## Step 2: self-serve credits (apply after Step 1)
| Program | What it likely gives us | Why it fits | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare for Startups** | $10,000 tier for bootstrapped companies (no funding needed) | Direct fit. Covers Pages, Workers, R2. Needs a live site and business email | https://www.cloudflare.com/hatch |
| **AWS Activate Founders** | $1,000 (up to $5,000 over time) | Self-funded is allowed. Needs a paid AWS account and a working website | https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/applying-for-aws-activate-credits-a-step-by-step-guide/ |
| **Microsoft for Startups** | $1,000 for 90 days, then $4,000 more after business verification | No investor needed for this tier | https://learn.microsoft.com/en-ie/answers/questions/5912941/eligibility-for-microsoft-for-startups-founders-hu |
| **Google for Startups Cloud (entry tier)** | up to about $2,000 for not-yet-funded companies | Entry tier only. Bigger tiers need institutional funding | https://dev.to/googleai/the-2026-ultimate-guide-to-google-for-startups-funding-accelerators-and-350k-in-credits-10ha |

Note: Only use credits if you actually need a back-end (accounts, database, saved applications). Do not build infrastructure just to spend credits. Credits expire (often in 12 months), and some programs forfeit unused balances.

## Step 3: cash (grants and competitions)
| Option | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hello Alice** profile | Good | Free to join. Matches US small businesses to grants. https://helloalice.com/funding/grants/ |
| **Campus and regional pitch competitions** | Good if a student is on the team | Examples: MIT $100K, e-Fest, Baylor, Dempsey. Eligibility depends on school and region. America's Startup closed Oct 8, 2026: watch for the next cycle |
| **State and city small-business microgrants** | Maybe | Search your state's economic development agency |
| **SCORE and Small Business Development Centers** | Good, free advice | From my own knowledge (not researched here): free mentoring on funding |

## Later, only if you get a partner or investor
- AWS Activate Portfolio ($100,000-$200,000), Microsoft with referral code (about $100,000), DigitalOcean (up to $100K), Vercel (up to $30K), Render (up to $100K): all mostly need an accelerator, incubator or VC partner.
- Join a school or local accelerator first, and these open up.

## Probably skip
| Program | Why |
|---|---|
| SBIR/STTR (NSF, up to about $305K) | Funds technical research and development. A swipe-to-apply app is a weak fit without a genuine technical innovation. Reauthorized April 2026 |
| FedEx Entrepreneur Fund | Appears closed, and narrow eligibility |
| Anthropic, OpenAI startup credits | Investor-gated in practice, and only useful if the app calls their APIs |
| Stripe Atlas ($500) | More than a plain LLC costs, and you may not need a Delaware C-Corp yet |

## Order of operations
1. This week: free hosting, campus outreach, the waitlist form.
2. Within about two weeks: decide the name, form the LLC, get EIN, domain and business email.
3. Then: apply to Cloudflare, AWS Activate, Microsoft and Google entry tier, and set up Hello Alice.
4. Ongoing: campus competitions and an accelerator or incubator referral.

## Honest expectations
- Realistic near-term value: roughly $1,000 to $15,000 in credits, plus small cash from competitions. Large credits and grants need an entity, a track record or a partner.
- Do not spend your $1,000 chasing credits. Spend it on the LLC, a domain and the trademark filing.
